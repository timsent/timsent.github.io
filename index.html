Research & Leadership

Focus areas

My work focuses on designing intelligent systems that integrate enterprise software, AI agents, multimodal models, and robotics into cohesive operating models.

01
Agentic AI and System 2 Reasoning for Robots September 2026

I extend Agentforce's Atlas Reasoning Engine into embodied AI, pairing NVIDIA's perception stack and visual language models with Cobalt's mobile robot platform.

The result is a Robot Safety Inspector proof of concept in which Atlas orchestrates autonomous room inspections for safety equipment, running fully local on DGX Spark. See the architecture →

02
Multi-Agent Collaboration June 2025

I trace the path from function calling to Agent2Agent, in the order the field actually built it, starting with OpenAI's tool calling and LangChain's work standardizing it across providers. From there I cover Mixture of Experts and Mixture of Agents, and how frameworks like LangGraph, CrewAI, MetaGPT, and AutoGen each let multiple agents collaborate.

I then cover Anthropic's Model Context Protocol and Google's Agent2Agent Protocol: what each one standardizes and why the two work together rather than compete. Read the deck →

03
Why Not Build an LLM? June 2024

I make the case for Retrieval-Augmented Generation over training a proprietary LLM from scratch. I walk through the cost curve behind frontier models, from hardware and energy to cloud rental and staff, and why language models have grown so quickly in size. I also trace the history of deep learning from the perceptron to the transformer era, including word embeddings, LSTMs, self attention, and BERT.

I cover the datasets and benchmarks the field trains and evaluates against, BloombergGPT as a finance focused example, and the RAG and vector database architecture that makes pretrained models ready for the enterprise without a training bill in the hundreds of millions of dollars. Read the deck →

04
The Language of Machine Learning November 2018

I put together a working vocabulary for machine learning, starting with what separates Artificial General Intelligence from the narrow systems we build today, then the three families of learning problems.

I cover regression, classification, the core algorithms and cost functions, and neural network fundamentals, plus the resources I recommend. Read the deck →

FIRST Robotics & Team 5199

As a programming mentor for FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) Team 5199, The Robot Dolphins From Outer Space, I developed and taught a structured curriculum covering coding principles, robot control systems, and advanced topics like AI-driven vision tracking.

A defining moment came when we migrated to a modern, command-based framework and introduced computer vision systems that unlocked full autonomous operation — rebuilding the entire codebase around structured, reusable patterns that scale season over season instead of resetting with each graduating class.

Standing with Team 5199 at the FIRST Championship, watching students operate under the pressure of the world's largest robotics stage — calm, coordinated, and confident — is the clearest proof that the programming culture we built together is durable and self-sustaining.
