Volunteering

FIRST Robotics & Team 5199

As a programming mentor for FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) Team 5199, The Robot Dolphins From Outer Space, I developed and taught a structured curriculum covering coding principles, robot control systems, and advanced topics like AI-driven vision tracking.

A defining moment came when we migrated to a modern, command-based framework and introduced computer vision systems that unlocked full autonomous operation — rebuilding the entire codebase around structured, reusable patterns that scale season over season instead of resetting with each graduating class.

Standing with Team 5199 at the FIRST Championship, watching students operate under the pressure of the world's largest robotics stage — calm, coordinated, and confident — is the clearest proof that the programming culture we built together is durable and self-sustaining.
